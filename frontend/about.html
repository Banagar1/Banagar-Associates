THE BANAGAR LEGACY

Welcome to Banagar Association

Banagar Association is committed to providing premium event spaces for families, organizations, and businesses looking for the perfect venue for their celebrations. Located in a peaceful environment, Banagar Lawns offers a spacious open area, modern facilities, guest accommodation, and a comfortable atmosphere suitable for events of all sizes.

Whether you are planning a wedding, reception, engagement ceremony, cultural event, birthday celebration, or corporate gathering, Banagar Lawns is the ideal destination for making every occasion special.

Our vision is to be the premier destination for life's most cherished milestones, setting the benchmark for premium hospitality and sophisticated gathering spaces. We continuously innovate our infrastructure to turn celebrations into pristine, cinematic, and timeless memories.

We operate with 100% Contract Integrity, keeping transparency at the forefront of every client relationship. Our team values exceptional customer care, structural safety, environmental cleanliness, and a frictionless experience from booking to execution.
